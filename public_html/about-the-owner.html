Meet the Owner

Engineer and cybersecurity graduate who learned operations on the mill floor, then fell in love with programming.

Kellie Williams

I am the owner of Trifecta.Systems LLC: a solo practice combining digital consulting and business consulting for small businesses and nonprofits. No agency bench. When you hire me, you get me.

From the mill to code:

  • Materials science background, then years at a steel mill managing an ISO-certified lab, quality, and shipping; later market analysis, technical product support at customer sites, and sales.
  • I left that career, returned to school for a B.S. in IT Cybersecurity, and fell in love with programming; that path is what built Trifecta.Systems.
  • I own projects end to end: troubleshooting, data-driven decisions, and delivery under real constraints.

Business consulting (recent expansion):

  • Intensive work with independent retail has pulled me deep into operations: org structure, personnel frameworks, SOPs, and product keep-or-cut decisions.
  • I also take on practical research: Colorado-relevant questions, policy context, vendor comparisons; as decision prep for owners (not legal advice).
  • Digital and business work reinforce each other: process without tools fails; tools without process gather dust.

How I work with sensitive systems and data:

  • Least privilege, documented process, and clear ownership over vague “trust us” security claims.
  • Privacy law awareness (GDPR/CCPA concepts) and data minimization as design inputs, not afterthoughts.
  • Write things down: SOPs, expectations, and technical decisions; so accountability is objective, not personal.

Education:

  • B.S. in IT Cybersecurity, Colorado State University (Cum Laude).
  • B.S. in Materials Science and Engineering, California State Polytechnic University, Pomona.

A jack of all trades is a master of none; but oftentimes better than a master of one. That breadth is intentional: web, data, security, operations, business structure, and AI literacy in one practitioner who can connect the dots.

What I enjoy most is working directly with owners; and watching an organization run more confidently once the operational and digital foundations are solid.
