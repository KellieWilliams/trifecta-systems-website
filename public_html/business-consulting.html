Business consulting from the trenches

Operations, structure, and hard decisions: for owners who need a thinking partner, not a slide deck agency.

What I Help With

Strategy and systems, under one roof.

Recently, I helped a fast-paced retail business transition from a single-owner model into a franchise framework. We tackled the structural planning, the documentation, and the messy reality of changing how a team works day-to-day. In a small business, problems don't happen in neat silos; so my approach doesn't either.

My background isn't typical for a digital consultant. I spent years in the steel industry managing an ISO-certified lab, overseeing quality control, and handling technical product support. That environment taught me how to build reliable systems that hold up under pressure. Today, I bring that same practical, data-driven approach to small businesses and nonprofits. Whether you are adapting to changing Colorado regulations, launching a new storefront, or simply testing new ways to operate, I focus on finding the best path forward.

Trifecta.Systems is a solo practice. When you hire me, you work directly with me. Because the business strategy and the digital tools are handled in one place, we can ensure your processes and your technology actually work together to make running your business easier.

Where I Add Leverage

  • Organizational structure & accountability: Creating clear roles, expectations, and handbooks that make accountability objective, not personal. I also build transition plans, brand guidelines, and asset lists for businesses moving into franchise or licensing models.
  • Operations & SOPs: Documenting how your business actually runs. I write standard operating procedures that are easy for staff and new licensees to follow: covering opening, closing, safety, and daily tasks; so things run smoothly even when you aren't in the room.
  • Product & offering decisions: Helping you move beyond "gut feel" when inventory or services grow. We can look at the data to decide what to keep, cut, or improve, build out retail pricing structures, or plan rollouts for new market opportunities.
  • Practical research support: Diving into the questions that slow you down. Whether it's vendor comparisons, navigating Colorado regulatory shifts, or summarizing policies into clear options, I do the prep work so you can make informed decisions. (Note: I am not an attorney; this is decision prep, not legal advice.)
  • Interim management & employee training: Stepping in as a temporary manager to keep operations moving, support your current team, and train new staff until a full-time replacement is ready to take the reins.
  • Strategic sounding board: Serving as a confidential, objective partner. When you need to pressure-test an idea, navigate a tricky personnel issue, or just talk through a hard decision before acting, I am here to help you weigh the options.
  • Change that sticks: Pairing process work with the digital tools that support it: like internal forms, dashboards, and access control; so the new, better way of working is also the easiest way.

Engagements are tailored to exactly what you need right now. Some clients start by asking for a website; others start with "we need to fix how this place runs" or "help us license this model." Both are right in my wheelhouse.
