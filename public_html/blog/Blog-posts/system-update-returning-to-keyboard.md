# System Update: Returning to the Keyboard

If you follow the timestamps on this site, you will notice a significant gap since my last post. While my goal is always to keep this space updated with fresh insights and technical guides, sometimes the physical world demands full priority over the digital one. 

The hiatus began late last year when I needed to step back to recover from a necessary surgery. Health and recovery must always come first, which meant completely clearing my plate for a period of time to ensure I could return at full capacity.

Immediately following that recovery, I took on an intensive, hands-on operational role for a fast-paced independent retail business. What began as a standard consulting and structuring project rapidly scaled into demanding, 60-plus-hour work weeks. For the better part of the last year, my days have been entirely consumed by the physical and structural demands of the business: building standard operating procedures from the ground up, restructuring personnel, and managing high-level organizational transitions. 

When you are that deeply immersed in the daily execution and management of a business, finding the bandwidth to step back, research, and write simply isn't feasible. 

However, there is a distinct advantage to spending a year in the operational trenches: it generates a massive amount of practical, battle-tested knowledge. Now that those structural frameworks are successfully implemented and my schedule is finally stabilizing, I am eager to start sharing those insights. 

In the coming weeks, you can expect new content drawing directly from this recent heavy lifting, starting with a deep dive into building corporate-level accountability structures within small, independent teams. 

It is good to be back at the keyboard.
