When Off-the-Shelf Is Not Enough

Sometimes a spreadsheet, a form tool, or a generic SaaS product leaves a gap. I build focused custom pieces: integrations, internal tools, site features, and automation that fit how your organization actually works.

AI is useful when the problem is clear and the data path is honest. I use generative tools offline for drafting, image assets, and assisted analysis; not as a novelty chat widget on your site. Solutions keep a person in the loop for decisions that matter. Treat prompts and outputs as potentially sensitive: minimize what you send to third-party models, and do not paste secrets or regulated data into public AI endpoints.

Typical engagements include workflow automation, embedding useful widgets on a site, connecting APIs, and producing on-brand creative assets: scoped, documented, and maintainable.

Where Custom & AI Help

  • Operations tooling: Checklists, intake forms, and small apps that encode your SOPs instead of living only in someone’s head.
  • Integrations: Connect the systems you already pay for so data moves once, accurately, and with access control in mind.
  • Content & creative assist: High-quality images and copy drafts for web and social: reviewed and edited before they go live.
  • Decision support: Lightweight analysis helpers that summarize or structure information; final calls stay with you and your staff.

Example creative output: a hyperrealistic frog generated with Midjourney for a visual showcase; useful when you need distinctive imagery without a full photo shoot.
